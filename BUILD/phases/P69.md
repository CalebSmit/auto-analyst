## Intent

The repeat path is copy-forward-and-shift-years. The rules are precise and were given precisely: the historical block is a fixed ten columns and the oldest falls off rather than the block growing; usually only the most recent five are filled; on the Input tab only the valuation date and the latest fiscal year end change, usually to one year later; and the WS line items are carried forward rather than re-decided, so the prior workbook is the chart of accounts. A line that does not match may mean the client changed something or that the firm grouped it differently last year, and the difference is a person's to call.

`B-036` 

## Tasks

- [x] A ten-wide year window rolls: the oldest drops, the window does not grow
- [x] The valuation date and latest fiscal year end roll forward a year, including across a leap day
- [x] Last year's line items are carried as this year's schema, and what does not match is reported both ways
- [x] Nothing here decides whether a mismatch is a client change or the firm's own prior grouping

## Exit criteria

*The phase closes when all of these are true and `gate close` says so.*

- Rolling a full ten-year window drops the oldest year and keeps the width at ten
- A window with fewer than ten years filled grows to the width and only then starts dropping
- Rolling 29 February forward a year lands on a real date rather than raising
- A prior line item with no match this year, and a new row matching no prior line item, are both reported and neither is resolved
