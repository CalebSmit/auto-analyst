## Intent

A guard rejects a skill's output and the step quarantines at once, though the recipe declares a region that exists to repair exactly that. RepairNeeded has a docstring describing the whole mechanism, a controller that can admit and exhaust it, a ceiling the compiler checks, and one caller - tests/p14/conftest.py. The Context Builder already assembles a repair_feedback section and nothing has ever passed one. Join the guards to the controller at the only place that can do it without leaking a client's figures, and carry the guard's failure to the model that has to act on it - because a repair pass that sends a byte-identical payload is a loop that looks like it works and cannot.
