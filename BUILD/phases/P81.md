## Intent

Hierarchy, not decoration. The checklist becomes the subject of the screen while there is work in it and yields when there is not; the steps are joined by a rail so seven marks read as one sequence; the dialogs are sectioned rather than stacked; emphasis is spent once per row instead of three times. And the page is held to the scales it is built on - every length from the spacing scale, every control wired one way - because the difference between composed and assembled is mostly that nothing was chosen by eye.
