## Intent

Read the unit the label declares, and do not apply the statement's scale to a row that is not in the statement's unit. Reading what a label says is not guessing - it is what two_sides does with the two labels a balance sheet writes - and leaving the scale on is not caution, it is a figure wrong by a factor of a thousand.
