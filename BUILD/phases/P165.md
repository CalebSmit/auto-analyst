## Intent

One place turns a figure into a number, and it asks the unit before it touches the scale. A share count is not restated to a currency scale - it passes through as it came and is listed as left alone - and a cell that is bound to one is refused with a sentence that says what the figure is a number of and what every input of the template is. The narrative prints a count as a count and never fills in a currency it has not got; the materiality test and the officer total refuse what is not money by decision rather than by a swallowed exception. Every site that multiplies reaches the one function, proved by making that function raise and watching each site fail - not by searching for a word.
