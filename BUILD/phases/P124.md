## Intent

Every procedure serializes its payload with indent=1, which spends a newline and a space on every key of every row. On a catalogue shaped like the real client folder that is 52 KB of whitespace in 269 KB - and the assembled model-load context is the step closest to the ceiling that refuses it. Compacting the separators changes no key, no value and no structure: the same object parses out the other side. What it buys is headroom on the one step most likely to refuse a real client, and it buys it without changing anything the model is asked to understand.
