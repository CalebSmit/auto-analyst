## Intent

The dashboard is something people enjoy being in: lit paper, weather under the masthead, a signature on every panel, a board that arrives, figures that land, a breathing dot on anything still happening, a bar that shimmers while it measures, feed lines that arrive, and the run's trail on the map travelling the way the run went - all of it in the brand's colours and on the two scales, and all of it stopped by the one reduced-motion rule. The Analytics board shows one skill as one row with its practice groups under it. And the runbook says exactly where the model-setup skill goes, what it must declare, which slot each of its paths fills, and where its client-naming half lives instead.
