## Intent

The form asks for the appraiser's vocabulary and omits the analyst's. A person setting up their first engagement should be told what is coming, should say repeat or new in those words, should not meet three different things called model, and should be able to undo what they made. None of this is new capability - it is the same settings, named and sequenced the way the person doing the work thinks about them.
