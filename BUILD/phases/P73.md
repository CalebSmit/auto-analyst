## Intent

An exception class is a claim that something can go wrong in a particular way. When production never constructs one, the claim is false and everything downstream of it - a handler, an event, a diagnostic code, a retry count in a recipe - is unreachable while looking entirely correct. Construction rather than raising is the test, because the three that are properly wired here reach the raise through a factory or a variable and a narrower rule would report them every time.
