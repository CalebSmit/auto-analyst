## Intent

Two things the setup surface gets wrong, both found by operating it rather than by reading it. Approving an already-approved recipe is a no-op in the register - same bytes, same approval, correctly write-once - but P57 sent it through the board anyway, so the governance chain gained an entry naming a person whose approval produced nothing and whose name the recipes list does not show. A record that says somebody approved something, beside a register that says somebody else did, is worse than either alone. And the engagement profile offered an auto-accept bar nobody had chosen.

## Tasks

- [x] Approving bytes that are already approved says so, and writes nothing
- [x] The auto-accept bar the profile form offers is the one a person set, at the conservative end

## Exit criteria

*The phase closes when all of these are true and `gate close` says so.*

- A second approval of the same file is refused with who approved it and when, and appends nothing to the governance chain
- A recipe edited since approval can still be approved, and that path is unchanged
- The profile skeleton offers 0.90 with owner-related always reviewed, and the decision is recorded
