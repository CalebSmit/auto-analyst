## Intent

The tool's claim is that an analyst can rely on the output without redoing it. Each of these three breaks that claim quietly - a wrong answer that passes every guard, or a confidence that is not warranted. Loud failures can wait; these cannot.

## Tasks

- [x] Materiality is magnitude, so a material add-back reaches a person whichever sign it carries
- [x] A statement whose rows hold two columns of figures stops for a person instead of being read as the prior year
- [x] A precedent that reads as naming a person is refused, because the store is read across clients on purpose
- [x] No client is left with no identifier at all when the distinctive-word filter drops every word of its name
- [x] The identifier scan refuses to report clean over a register with nothing in it

## Exit criteria

*The phase closes when all of these are true and `gate close` says so.*

- A material add-back routes to review whether its figure is positive or negative, and a test proves it by failing without the fix
- A comparative statement is flagged and escalated; a single-column one and ordinary prose are not
- A rationale naming a person is refused into the Precedent Store, an ordinary add-back rationale is not, and neither the event nor the diagnostic quotes what was found
- Every client name yields at least one identifier for the masker
