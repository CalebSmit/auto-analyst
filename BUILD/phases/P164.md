## Intent

Read the direction a label states and say when the figure contradicts it. Total assets is not negative, net cash used in financing is not positive, an accumulated deficit is not positive - these are statements about the table and not about the business, and that line is the whole of the rule: a label that could honestly go either way says nothing. A contradiction withdraws the arithmetic cover of the block it is in, because a proof that passes a wholly negated column proves nothing about any row of it. Report, never repair, and calibrate every phrase against the three real filings before it is kept.

- [x] Report every row whose sign contradicts its own label, beside the footing report, in the footing vocabulary
- [x] A contradiction withdraws the cover of its block: no ordinal in it counts as proved by arithmetic
- [x] Stay silent on a column headed as a change, because there every sign is a movement

## Exit criteria

*The phase closes when all of these are true and `gate close` says so.*

- A balance sheet column with every sign inverted is reported, with the rows named, and carries no arithmetic cover
- Over every row of the three public filings the rule contradicts nothing, and the number of rows it reads at all is asserted so its coverage cannot be overstated
- A label that can honestly carry either sign - gross profit, net income, cash at the end of a period, a proceed inside a roll-forward - is not read, proved by the rows on the filings that would have fired
- The three tie-outs still say what they said on the unchanged corpus
