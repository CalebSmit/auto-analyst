## Intent

A file telling two decimal stories decides nothing and says so, and a cell that does not parse under the settled convention is reported rather than reinterpreted. A number with an unbalanced parenthesis is refused, not read positive, so the footing catches the dropped figure; a trailing minus and a unicode minus are read as the negatives they are.
