# QC conventions

> **Skeleton.** Written by the practice group. See README.md.

## Tie-out

Every figure in a deliverable is recomputed, never restated. A recomputed total that does
not match the stated total is a review item carrying both numbers and the delta.

## Readiness

The readiness field cannot say approved. It says `items_outstanding` or
`no_automated_issues_found`, and nothing else.
